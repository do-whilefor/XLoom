# Built-in method guidance

These 13 cards are condensed adaptations of the user-supplied Webounty method
library. Each JSON card contains execution guidance and a shorter review.
`catalog.json` provides selection descriptions. `provenance.json` retains the
original card paths and SHA-256 hashes, plus Webounty's upstream attribution
records. Upstream paths are historical source references, not installation
dependencies. Hashes identify source bytes; they do not verify case claims.

Webounty credits the user-supplied Wiki/RAG prototype for method-card organization
and Jase-SecKit's Web-Vulnhunt for portions of the method guidance. The relevant
Web-Vulnhunt MIT notice is preserved in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt):
Copyright (c) 2026 w1th0ut (U-Sec / 无界安全).

Xloom retains comparison validity, conditional refutation, missing prerequisites
and concrete reopening conditions. The impact card uses Xloom's existing rating
contract. No Webounty session engine, external network scripts, historical target
claims or CVSS calculator is included. Source narratives and attribution metadata
are not injected into model prompts or registered as task evidence.
